## 1. Problem summary

**What is happening now?**

Legacy Trust Bank has spent years modernising customer-facing banking journeys, but debt recovery never made it onto the same roadmap. Today, more than 50 representatives are still working across spreadsheets, email trails, and a 20-year-old collections database to manage over 100,000 delinquent accounts. The result is more than just frustration: missed follow-ups, duplicated activity, and inconsistent status updates are now directly affecting recoveries, operational capacity, and leadership confidence.

**Why is it failing at scale?**

The legacy infrastructure and patchworked workarounds have hit a breaking point as account volumes grow. It is a process that depends heavily on individual memory, manual reconciliation, and duplicated effort. As delinquent account volumes increased, those workarounds stopped scaling. Managers lost visibility of which follow-ups had happened, representatives developed inconsistent ways of updating statuses, and customers were often pushed into slow, repetitive contact journeys. Skilled representatives waste time on manual tracking and low-complexity cases instead of focusing on high-value accounts requiring human judgment.

**Why does it matter financially and operationally?**

Delayed actions and inconsistent follow-up are contributing to an estimated 15% revenue loss (Note: Sourced directly from leadership interview notes with Finance & Compliance Director Daniel Okoye). Wasted handling time and duplicated effort inflate operational overhead across the 50+ rep team.

**What evidence do we need before approving Phase 1?**

An audit-proof business case showing that a self-service portal resolves core operational friction without introducing delivery or compliance risk. Present data-backed proof of current operational waste and a clear operating model with explicit escalation boundaries. We want to answer the following questions:

- Where does the current process break down? 
- Which journeys are suitable for automation? 
- Does the Phase 1 portal pay back within 12 months?

## 3. Discovery questions

- Where do manual handoffs, spreadsheet updates, and duplicate actions create handling delays or missed follow-ups?
- Which delinquency stages, product types, and customer segments are eligible for self-service balance confirmation and payment plans without representative intervention?
- What specific operational, financial, and team pain points do Amina, Daniel, Gareth, and customers experience in the As-Is process?
- What combination of capacity savings and improved recovery yields is required to achieve full portal payback within 12 months?
- What metrics or evidence can be used to quantify operational waste and potential automation benefits?
- What are the potential risks and compliance issues associated with the current process?
- What legacy constraints affect implementation effort and rep adoption?
