## Step 4: Top 3 justification

- JTBD-01 "When I need to review or settle an outstanding debt, I want clear, real-time access to my current balance details, so that I can efficiently manage my obligations without repeated calls or confusion."

Customers currently lack real-time visibility into their outstanding balances, driving unnecessary phone interactions and creating friction during the debt settlement process. Establishing self-service early empowers customers with control and transparency, which directly improves repayment speed and operational efficiency. Data from delinquent_accounts_export.csv shows that 77% of sample customers are prime candidates for self-service. This represents £2,342,405.98 in overdue balances (77% of the sample's total overdue amount). Phase 1 scope must prioritize the Customer Debtor Portal with core functionalities for real-time balance lookups, account status views, and automated payment arrangement workflows.

- JTBD-03 "When I complete a case interaction or update an account status, I want my notes and status updates to be accessible across all relevant systems, so that I can devote my working hours to resolving complex cases rather than repeating manual administrative tasks."

Frontline representatives are suffering from heavy administrative drag caused by manual double-entry across disparate legacy systems and spreadsheets. Eliminating this manual overhead immediately reclaims representative capacity to handle complex, high-value cases. recovery_activity_tracker.csv reveals that 25.2% of all activities (2,492 logs) originate from spreadsheets, taking 17,293 total minutes. Specifically, spreadsheet_reconcile tasks alone account for 1,458 activities (14.7%) and ~218.7 hours. At £22.00/hour (FA-01), this manual re-keying wastes ~£4,811 in labor costs within this sample dataset alone.

- JTBD-05 "When an account requires scheduled follow-up or debtor outreach, I want a reliable way to ensure scheduled follow-ups actually happen, so that overdue accounts receive timely intervention and do not age indefinitely in stagnant queues."

Lack of automated follow-up enforcement causes cases to stall in static queues without timely intervention. Enforcing follow-up schedules prevents debt decay, reduces leakage, and ensures routine interventions occur before accounts become non-recoverable. In recovery_activity_tracker.csv, 14.8% of activities (1,465 logs) have a blank next_follow_up_date, and 12.9% (1,275 logs) end with outcome_code = 'next_action_unclear'. This validates finance assumption FA-06, which models an indicative 14.0% missed or delayed follow-up rate. Phase 1 should incorporate mandatory follow-up triggers and automated SLA routing in the back-office queue. Additionally, the customer portal can directly mitigate this job by offering self-service appointment/callback scheduling to ensure explicit follow-up dates are set automatically.
