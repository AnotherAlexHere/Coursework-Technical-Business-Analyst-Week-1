# Current Process Experiences

The current process experiences highlight the challenges faced by frontline representatives and the inefficiencies in handling case interactions and account updates. Manual double-entry across disparate legacy systems and spreadsheets creates significant administrative drag, consuming valuable time that could be spent on complex, high-value cases. Additionally, the lack of automated follow-up enforcement leads to stalled cases and delayed interventions, impacting overall recovery performance.


## Customer Experience
Customers often experience delays and inconsistencies in account updates and case resolutions due to the manual and fragmented nature of the current processes. The lack of seamless integration between systems means that customers may need to provide the same information multiple times, leading to frustration and a perception of inefficiency. Furthermore, missed or delayed follow-ups can result in overdue accounts not being addressed promptly, meaning customers paying more interest than necessary and potentially facing negative impacts on their credit scores.

## Frontline Representative Experience
Frontline representatives face significant administrative burdens due to the need to manually enter data across multiple legacy systems and spreadsheets. This double-entry not only consumes valuable time but also increases the risk of errors and inconsistencies. The lack of automated follow-up enforcement further complicates their workflow, as representatives must constantly track and manage follow-up schedules to ensure timely interventions. These challenges hinder their ability to focus on complex, high-value cases. That being said, some worry about job security due to potential automation of these administrative tasks.

## Management Experience

Managers face challenges in overseeing case progress and ensuring timely interventions due to the fragmented and manual nature of the current processes. The lack of real-time visibility into case updates and follow-up schedules makes it difficult to monitor performance and enforce accountability. Additionally, the administrative overhead on frontline representatives can lead to delays and errors, further complicating management efforts and impacting overall recovery performance.


# Automation opportunities

Automation can significantly alleviate the administrative burdens faced by frontline representatives and improve overall process efficiency. By integrating systems and automating data entry, representatives can focus on complex, high-value cases rather than repetitive manual tasks. Automated follow-up enforcement ensures timely interventions, reducing the risk of overdue accounts and improving recovery performance. Additionally, real-time visibility into case updates and follow-up schedules can enhance management oversight and accountability, leading to more effective and efficient operations.
